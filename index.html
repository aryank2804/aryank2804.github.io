Aryan Khurana

I am a final year undergraduate at BITS Pilani, where I am majoring in Electrical and Electronics Engineering. Currently, I am a research intern at Inria Sophia Antipolis with the STARS team, advised by Dr. François Brémond, where I work on video generation and understanding.

Previously, I was a research intern at NTNU, advised by Prof. Kiran Raja, where I worked on generative models for synthetic face images and their applications in identity documents. I was also a research intern at the University of Edinburgh, advised by Prof. Vaishak Belle, where I worked on interpretability, fairness, and trustworthiness in large language models. I was also part of the MultiNet team at the Manifold Research Group.

I am broadly interested in large language and multimodal generative models, and interpretability. Feel free to reach out if you'd like to discuss research or just chat!
