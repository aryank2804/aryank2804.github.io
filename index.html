Aryan Khurana

I'm an undergraduate at BITS Pilani, studying Electrical and Electronics Engineering. I'm currently a research intern with the STARS team at Inria in Sophia Antipolis, where I work on video generation with François Brémond.

Previously, I was a research intern with Kiran Raja at NTNU, on generating and bias-correcting synthetic face images for ICAO-compliant identity documents, and with Vaishak Belle at the University of Edinburgh, on using interpretability to improve fairness and trustworthiness in LLMs. I was also part of the MultiNet team at the Manifold Research Group, benchmarking multimodal foundation models for zero-shot generalization.

I'm broadly interested in generative models, language and multimodal models, and interpretability.
